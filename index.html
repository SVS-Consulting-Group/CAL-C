Our Training Courses

Software Courses

This course provides a thorough introduction to software development, equipping students with the skills and knowledge needed to design, code, test, and debug software applications. Through a blend of theoretical concepts and hands-on practice, students will learn to develop efficient and functional software solutions.

Hardware Courses

This course offers a comprehensive introduction to the field of hardware engineering, covering fundamental concepts and practical skills required to design, build, and troubleshoot computer hardware. Students will gain hands-on experience with various hardware components and systems, learning how they interact and function together.

Overview

  • Punjab Information & Communication Technology Corporation Ltd (Punjab Infotech) is a Punjab Government Undertaking that has been successfully spearheading the Software Training revolution through a strong network of CAL-C Computer Education Centers in Punjab since 1988. Punjab Infotech is the premier agency of the state government in facilitating establishment of IT and IT enabled service's (ITES) industry in Punjab, and training human resources for this industries. After the success in Software skill set, CAL-C took a step further in the field of Advanced Hardware & Networking in 2003.
  • CAL-C provides focused Software, Hardware, Networking and ITES courses with technical inputs from industry leaders. CAL-C has an extensive network of training partner centres in the region to provide quality computer training vis-a-vis industry requirement. More than 80+ CAL-C Centres in Punjab & Himachal Pradesh. PGDCA course by Punjab Infotech accredited by Punjab State Board of Technical Education (PSBTE).
  • Apart from regular courses Punjab Infotech has also provided free training in various sponsored schemes of GoI and State Government eg WISHScheme, SC BPL training etc.

Our Objectives

  • Spread IT awareness to the youth of Punjab & India.
  • Provide professional training in IT as per the changing industry requirement to improve employability to the youth.
  • Maintain high quality of training.
